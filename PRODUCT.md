# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Watchlist is for people maintaining a personal movie and series collection across desktop and mobile. They use it to decide what to watch, track progress, monitor releases, and keep ratings and notes close to the titles they care about.

## Product Purpose

Watchlist turns a personal watchlist into a focused media database. Success means users can move quickly between their movie collection, series progress, upcoming releases, discovery feeds, and detail pages without losing context.

## Brand Personality

Focused, cinematic, and precise. The interface should feel calm and personal while retaining the density and reliability of a well-kept database.

## Anti-references

- No large redesign; preserve the current compact style.
- Do not imitate a generic streaming-service home screen.
- Avoid decorative UI that competes with poster artwork or slows routine collection management.

## Design Principles

- Organize around the content users own: movies and series are primary destinations.
- Keep collection state and upcoming releases one clear interaction apart.
- Preserve context through stable URLs, predictable back navigation, and persistent controls.
- Let artwork carry atmosphere while controls remain restrained and functional.
- Prefer familiar, accessible product patterns over novel navigation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Maintain keyboard access, visible focus states, semantic navigation and tabs, sufficient contrast across every theme, touch-friendly controls, and reduced-motion behavior for non-essential transitions.
